# Security policy

PassGuardian handles secrets: security reports take priority over everything else.

## Status
The project is in an early phase and **has not been audited**. Do not use it for passwords that matter.

## How to report a vulnerability
**Do not open a public issue.** Use GitHub's *Report a vulnerability* feature (Security advisories)
or write to `sukhdev.mohan@gmail.com`.

Please include: description, steps to reproduce, estimated impact, version.

## What we commit to
- Acknowledgement within 5 working days.
- Assessment and a fix plan within 30 days.
- Coordinated disclosure and credit (if you wish).

## Scope
Cryptography and vault format, authentication, sync, server, clients. Out of scope: attacks that already
require full control of the user's unlocked device.
